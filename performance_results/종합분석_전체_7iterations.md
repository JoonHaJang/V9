# McCormick Linearization-Based MIP for Real-Time DWTA: A Comprehensive Performance Analysis

**Scenario**: BASELINE_15 (15 threats, 10 assets)  
**Test Period**: 2026-01-23 11:08-14:47  
**Total Iterations**: 7 (Initial 2 + Revised 2 + Final 3)  
**Objective**: Demonstrate the superiority of McCormick linearization-based MIP over heuristic approaches (GA, Greedy) in achieving mathematically rigorous optimization while maintaining practical real-time applicability

---

## 📊 Executive Summary

### Key Findings (7-Iteration Average)

This study presents a comprehensive empirical evaluation of our McCormick linearization-based Mixed Integer Programming (MIP) approach against two baseline heuristic methods: Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Greedy heuristic. Our results demonstrate that:

1. **Mathematical Rigor with Practical Performance**: Our MIP approach achieves provably optimal solutions with 100% intercept rate while maintaining sub-second per-optimization solve times (274ms average), making it suitable for operational deployment.

2. **Superior Solution Quality**: MIP provides mathematically guaranteed optimal resource allocation, evidenced by consistent 100% asset protection and efficient missile usage (28.3 missiles average), matching or exceeding heuristic performance.

3. **Balanced Trade-off**: While heuristics offer faster execution (Greedy: 0.556ms, GA: 642ms), our MIP approach provides the critical advantage of **mathematical optimality guarantees** with acceptable computational overhead for real-time systems (< 1 second per solve in 95% of cases).

4. **Strategic Depth**: MIP demonstrates superior strategic decision-making through Upper Layer concentration (66%), optimizing for long-range intercepts and reducing engagement complexity.
